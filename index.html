STELLAR DRIFT

A Newtonian space flight simulator

Fly a fusion-drive ship through the real Solar System and numerous nearby star systems, including their exoplanets, based on real discoveries. Real orbits, real gravity, real distances.

  • You start docked at Gateway Station above Earth with a small courier and 3,000 credits
  • Trade real and exotic commodities between 15 stations to earn credits, then buy bigger ships
  • Pick a destination and let the autopilot fly you there, orbit, land, or dock
  • Or fly by hand: W A S D R F to thrust, arrows or drag to steer
  • Use the jump drive to cross interstellar space

Starting a new career erases this one. Save it to a file first? You can load it again later with IMPORT SAVE in the help panel.
