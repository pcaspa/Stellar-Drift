The game

Real physics. A whole neighbourhood of stars.

Every planet, moon and station follows its real orbit, and your ship obeys Newton. Pick a destination and let the autopilot fly the burn, or take the stick and do it yourself.

FLY

Real orbits & gravity

The Sun, planets, moons and asteroids move on their true orbits. Orbit, land on any world with terrain, dock with spinning stations, or fly every burn by hand.

EXPLORE

30+ star systems

The Solar System, ten nearby stars with known exoplanets, and twenty uncharted systems whose untouched asteroid belts hide the rarest finds of all.

JUMP

Interstellar jump drive

Align with the jump vector, burn at full power and the drive spools up — the stars stretch into streaks and you arrive light-years away.

TRADE

Living markets

27 commodities across 25 stations and ground spaceports. Prices drift, react to your trades, and you can compare markets across the stars.

MINE

Drill for fortune

Scan asteroids and moons for deposits, land on the richest site and lower the drills — from water ice to Iridium, Xenocrystals and Exotic Matter.

WORK

Contracts

Courier sealed cargo, carry passengers, source goods to order, or fly to the edge of the map for the Orbital Survey Office.

COMMAND

Run a fleet

Buy extra ships, hire crews and set them on trade loops between stations. They wire you the profit — if they make it.

SURVIVE

Raiders & running costs

Valuable cargo draws pirates: pay, out-run them, or reach a station's patrols. Crews and maintenance cost money every day you fly.
